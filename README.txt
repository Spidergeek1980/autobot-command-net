AUTOBOT COMMAND NET — AB-2026
Unit DL-2026 · Teletraan-1 uplink

WHAT THIS IS
A single-file Cybertronian communicator. Twelve Autobots plus Teletraan-1, each with a
procedural holo portrait, a distinct synthetic voice, and personality-specific dialogue.

VOICE RECOGNITION
Tap LISTEN and speak. Chrome, Edge and Safari (iOS 14.5+) can hear you. The mic needs
https (or localhost) — open the GitHub Pages link, not the local file, on a phone.
Say things like:
  "Optimus, status report"     "Bumblebee, what do you see"
  "roll call"                  "transform and roll out"
  "Grimlock, are you hungry"   "hey Jazz, tell me a joke"
  "Autobots, sound off"        "Arcee, cover me"
Tap AUTO to answer everything on the net, or leave it off to answer only when a unit
is named. Tap NET for ambient chatter from the roster. Tap VOICE to mute/unmute.

iOS Safari: Share, "Add to Home Screen".
